## EN – Device Profiles & Treatments

### Fotona DYNAMIS MAX (Er:YAG 2940 nm + Nd:YAG 1064 nm)
**How it works:** Dual-wavelength platform for fractional/full-field resurfacing (Er:YAG) and deep vascular/pigment/hair & bulk heating (Nd:YAG).  
**Why:** Selective photothermolysis + controlled thermal remodeling → collagen reorganization and targeted correction.  
**Applications:** Fotona4D®, SmoothEye®, LipLase®, fractional resurfacing, vascular/pigment, TightSculpting®, hair reduction.  
**Positioning:** Latest configuration for **surgery-like** remodeling without incisions; choreographed with other devices same day.

### AirPod Revive Hydroxy (mHBOT + H₂)
**How it works:** Mild hyperbaric oxygen (1.1–1.3 ATA) + molecular hydrogen inhalation.  
**Why:** ↑ plasma O₂ → better tissue oxygenation; H₂ acts as selective antioxidant modulator → recovery & anti-inflammatory support.  
**Applications:** Post-procedure comfort, jet-lag reset, cognitive clarity, athletic recovery.  
**Positioning:** Unique day-of-procedure add-on in Milan.

### Ammortal Chamber (PEMF/PEF + Red/NIR PBM + H₂ + Vibro-Acoustic)
**How it works:** Multi-wave PEMF/PEF; full-body PBM (660/850 nm); H₂ inhalation; vibro-acoustic sessions.  
**Why:** Membrane potential modulation + mitochondrial ATP upshift + redox balance + autonomic reset.  
**Applications:** Stress/sleep recovery, pain/DOMS support, wellness optimization between aesthetic sessions.  
**Positioning:** Signature KinAura social-wellness experience in Milan.

### HydraFacial Syndeo MD
**How it works:** Vortex-Fusion™ (cleanse/peel, vacuum extraction, booster infusion).  
**Why:** Immediate hydration and polished texture via enhanced permeability post-exfoliation.  
**Applications:** Congestion, dehydration, uneven texture, neck/chest/back maintenance, pre-event glow.  
**Positioning:** MD-grade with VISIA-matched boosters; pair with PBM for glow.
