## EN – Device Profiles & Treatments

### VISIA-7 Skin Analysis System
**How It Works:**  
7th-generation 3D facial imaging with AI wrinkle detection, aging and injectable simulation, and RBX® technology for vascular/pigment analysis. TruSkin Age® compares skin health to chronological age.

**Why It Works:**  
Combines the world's largest skin analysis database with advanced imaging to accurately assess and track skin condition over time.

**Treatments/Applications:**  
- Pre-treatment mapping for all skin protocols.  
- Progress tracking for anti-aging, pigmentation, vascular issues, acne.  
- Personal skincare/procedure planning.

**KinAura Exclusivity:**  
Only VISIA-7 in Milan within a luxury regenerative clinic.

### Sciton mJOULE Platform (BBL HERO, MOXI, SkinTyte)
**How It Works:**  
- **BBL HERO™:** High-energy broadband light targets pigment, vascular lesions, and stimulates collagen.  
- **MOXI™:** 1927nm fractionated thulium laser resurfaces epidermis.  
- **SkinTyte™:** Infrared for skin tightening.

**Why It Works:**  
Delivers selective photothermolysis to pigment and hemoglobin, while non-ablative resurfacing stimulates collagen.

**Treatments/Applications:**  
- Pigmentation, sun damage, redness, vascular lesions.  
- Skin rejuvenation (face/body).  
- Acne (Forever Clear™), hair removal, tightening.

**KinAura Exclusivity:**  
Only Milan clinic with combined MOXI + BBL HERO "Light & Bright" protocols.

---

### InMode Ignite Platform (Morpheus8 Burst, FaceTite, BodyTite)
**How It Works:**  
- **Morpheus8 Burst:** RF microneedling at multiple depths for collagen remodeling and resurfacing.  
- **FaceTite:** RF-assisted lipolysis for facial contour.  
- **BodyTite:** RF-assisted tightening for large body zones.

**Why It Works:**  
Delivers controlled thermal injury at precise depths to stimulate collagen and contract tissue.

**Treatments/Applications:**  
- Wrinkle reduction, tightening, scar remodeling.  
- Fat reduction with skin contraction.  
- Jawline/neck contouring.

**KinAura Exclusivity:**  
First in Milan with Morpheus8 Burst Deep.

**Why It Works:**

**Treatments/Applications:**  
- Enhanced PRP/exosome injections.  
- Combination with microneedling, lasers, hair restoration.

**KinAura Exclusivity:**  
Only clinic in Milan conditioning biologics pre-application.
